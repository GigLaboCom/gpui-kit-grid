title: Sponsor
description: Support the development of gpui-kit-grid.
---

gpui-kit-grid is free for any use, under the Apache License 2.0. If it is useful to you or your company, consider [supporting its development](https://buymeacoffee.com/desakov).

Support pays for the time the [roadmap](../roadmap/) needs: the polish stage, documentation, more languages, and keeping the grid in step with gpui-kit and GPUI as they move.

Other ways to help cost nothing:

- try the grid in your application, and [open an issue](https://github.com/GigLaboCom/gpui-kit-grid/issues) for what breaks or is missing;
- report how it behaves on Windows and Linux, and with screen readers;
- tell others about it.
