The repository's examples range from a minimal quick start to full
applications built on the grid, and include the headless benchmark. Each
one runs with `cargo run` from a checkout of the repository; the
[Getting started](../start/) page lists what a checkout needs. The trading
examples and most of the others load their fonts first, with
`python3 -I script/fetch-example-fonts`; orbit and transit fetch their
data once with `script/fetch-orbit-data` and `script/fetch-transit-data`.

A demo in the browser downloads about 7 MB of WebAssembly, only when you
ask for it, and runs one at a time on this page. **Open full page** shows
the same example on a page of its own, with the switcher to the others.
Prices in the market examples and everything in ops are simulated; the
[licences](../licenses/) page says where the other data comes from.
